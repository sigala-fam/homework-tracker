Why TaskNest exists

Made by a student, for students.

I built TaskNest for myself and my friends. There are some great homework trackers out there, but most of them cost money, have limits, or are missing something I needed. So I made one that has everything I wanted, and kept it free.

The Plan feature is the part I care about most. When you're stuck and can't decide which task to start with, TaskNest shows you what to do first, so getting started is the easy part.

My goal is simple: help people get their work done, stay organized, and feel a lot less stressed.

And if TaskNest is missing something you need, tell me. Signed-in users can send a feature suggestion right from the app. TaskNest is built to grow with the people who use it.

— the student who made TaskNest

Meet Midnight

Your little study buddy

Midnight is TaskNest's pixel black cat. She lives on a shelf by her window on the sign-in screen, where she walks around, grooms her paws, lounges, and curls up for a nap. Her window even shows sunny, rainy, evening or night skies. Don't want a cat on your homework? You can switch her off in Settings.
