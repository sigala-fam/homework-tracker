What's New

Every TaskNest update, newest first. Small updates add .1 (like 2.3 → 2.4). Big ones get a whole new number (like 1.3 → 2.0).

Latest: Version 2.4

TaskNest now works fully on phones and tablets, and changing your name looks a lot nicer.

Version 2.4

October 14, 2026

  • On phones and tablets, buttons that used to need a mouse (like deleting a card or adding steps) now always show
  • The calendar's color key now shows on phones, right under the calendar
  • The weekly view on phones now shows your clubs and personal events too
  • A new, nicer box for changing your name
  • This What's New page!

Version 2.3

October 11, 2026

  • A brand-new front page that shows what TaskNest can do
  • Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Cookies and Contact pages
  • Stronger passwords when you sign up, with a meter that shows how strong yours is
  • Change your password from inside the app
  • Suggest a feature: send us your ideas from the profile menu
  • A friendly page for when a link goes nowhere

Version 2.2

August 4, 2026

  • Meet Midnight, the pixel cat who hangs out on the sign-in screen
  • Boards now work fully on touchscreens: drag with your finger, pinch to zoom, long-press to move things
  • Smooth little animations all around the app
  • Board cards show what's waiting inside, and each board can have its own color

Version 2.1

June 30, 2026

  • Big tasks now get split across a few days in your Daily Schedule, labeled "Part 1 of 2" and so on

Version 2.0: The Big Board Update

June 19, 2026

  • Your board is now a giant canvas you can move around in any direction
  • Sticky notes, pictures, tables and lines to connect things
  • Make as many boards as you want, with a home screen to switch between them
  • Drag columns, classes and tasks anywhere you like
  • New boards can start from a ready-made layout

Version 1.3

June 17, 2026

  • Repeating events: every week, or several days in a row
  • Quickly add an event straight from a day on the calendar

Version 1.2

June 2, 2026

  • Smarter planning that spreads your homework across your free days
  • Set the hours you like to do homework
  • The calendar shows which days to work on something, not just when it's due
  • Alerts for homework that's due today
  • Easier to read, with better colors and contrast

Version 1.1

May 27, 2026

  • A whole new look, with purple colors and a dark mode
  • The TaskNest name and logo
  • A profile menu with everything in one place
  • Add your own clubs with their own colors, and give events start and end times

Version 1.0

May 24, 2026

  • TaskNest is born! A homework board and a calendar
  • Log in to keep your homework saved on every device
  • Works on phones too
